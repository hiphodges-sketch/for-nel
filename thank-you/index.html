I wanted to say thank you properly. For all of it. You’ve had enough of my words this month so most of this is pictures. I went back through everything we ever sent each other to build it. An entire weekend and 46,000 texts and I don’t regret a minute of it.

Swipe the photos. You don’t have to reply to any of it. It’s just thank you.
